Upload log files from Linux servers, web servers or AI agents. The lab finds suspicious activity and explains each finding: what happened, why it matters, and what to check next.

Building a classroom lab

  1. Load your logs in the box below. Any mix of syslog, auth.log, Apache or Nginx, JSON lines or CSV works.
  2. Look through the findings, then open Instructor tools at the bottom of this page.
  3. Add custom rules if you need them, write or generate questions, and use Preview as a student to test the lab.
  4. Click Download lab file and share it with your students. They open it here with Open a lab file.

Instructor tools: custom rules and lab builder

Load your own logs above, add rules for patterns the built-in rules miss, then turn the logs into a lab that students open in this page. Everything stays in the browser.

1. Custom detection rules

A text match flags every event containing a word or pattern. A threshold flags repeated matches from one IP address or user within a time window, like the brute-force rule.

2. Lab builder

The lab file contains the logs currently loaded, your custom rules, the threshold settings and your questions. Students open it with "Open a lab file". Answers are stored scrambled (hashed), which discourages peeking but is not real security.
